What these runs establish

  • Real model responses, actual function execution, genuine source data, and versioned evidence.
  • A nine-question official Coffee pilot; USGS revision and replay demonstrations; an authored weather service pilot.
  • Failures remain visible alongside repairs and later successes.

What remains open

  • These are small pilots, not the paper's aggregate benchmark results.
  • Two weather final requests are still untouched. Weather candidates were not automatically promoted.
  • A second materially different MCP surface, broader workflow evaluation, and arbitrary semantic migrations remain unverified.
  • LangSmith cloud tracing is not configured. Local artifacts are the evidence source.
